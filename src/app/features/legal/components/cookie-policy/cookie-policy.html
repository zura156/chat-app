Other scripts

Cloudflare Web Analytics loads from static.cloudflareinsights.com and reports each page you open — including moves between screens inside the app — with the page address, the page you came from, your browser and device type, and how long the page took to load. It sets no cookies and writes nothing to local or session storage. We use it to see which parts of the Service are used and which are slow. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Your control

You can clear or block all of this in your browser's settings. Blocking cookies will sign you out and stop you signing back in; clearing storage resets your display preferences. A content or tracker blocker can stop the analytics script without affecting anything else.
