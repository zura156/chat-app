2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: first and last name, username, email address and password. We store the password only as a one-way hash, never the password itself.
  • Profile details you choose to add: a bio, a profile picture and a cover photo.
  • What you send: the text of your messages and their edits, and the images, videos, voice messages and files you attach, including each file's name, type and size. Group names and group pictures you set.
  • Settings: your privacy choices, the people you block, the conversations you mute, and two-factor authentication settings.

If you sign in with Google or GitHub

We receive your account identifier at that provider, your verified email address and your name (and, from GitHub, your GitHub username). We use them to create or sign in to your account. We do not receive your password for that provider, and we do not post anything on your behalf.

Information created as you use the Service

  • Conversation records: which conversations you are in, who else is in them, when each message was sent, and whether it was delivered and read.
  • Presence: whether you are online, and when you were last seen.
  • Security records: for each device you are signed in on, the IP address and browser or device description (user agent) it signed in from, and when it was created and last used. We also keep your last sign-in time and a count of recent failed sign-in attempts.
  • Server logs: records of errors, which may include an account identifier, and short-lived rate-limit counters keyed by IP address or account.
  • Usage statistics: a small script from Cloudflare Web Analytics reports each page you open, including moves between screens inside the app, with the page address, the page you came from, your browser and device type, and how long the page took to load. Page addresses can contain the ID of a conversation or of a profile you are viewing, but never message content. The script sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device.

On your device

A few cookies keep you signed in and protect your account, and your browser remembers display preferences such as your theme. All of them are needed for the Service to work; none track you. The analytics script above stores nothing in your browser. The Cookies & Storage page lists each one.

4. Who can see it

Other users

  • Your profile. Any signed-in user can find you in the user list and in search, and see your name, username, cover photo and the date you joined. Your bio, profile picture, online status and last-seen time follow your privacy settings: everyone, only people you share a conversation with, or nobody. One exception today: unless your online status is set to nobody, people you share a conversation with are also sent your last-seen time each time you go online or offline, even if last seen is hidden. Your email address is never shown to other users.
  • Your messages. Everyone in a conversation — including people added to a group later — can see the messages and files sent to it, when they were sent, and whether they have been read. Group members can see who else is in the group.
  • Contact. Any user can start a conversation with you or add you to a group unless one of you has blocked the other. Blocking hides each of you from the other's search and profile view, and stops either of you messaging the other directly or adding the other to a group.

Service providers

5. Security and encryption

Traffic between your device and our servers is encrypted with TLS. Messages and files are not end-to-end encrypted: they are stored on our servers in a form the operator of the Service could read. Please keep that in mind before sending anything highly sensitive.

Other safeguards: passwords are hashed; documents you attach are scanned for malware, and images, video and audio are re-encoded, before anyone receives them; files sent in conversations are kept in private storage and shown only through signed links that expire after a few hours; and you can turn on two-factor authentication and see and sign out the devices on your account in Settings → Security.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your data at risk, we will tell you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

6. How long we keep it

  • Your account, profile and messages are kept until you delete them or delete your account.
  • A deleted message loses its text and attachments immediately. An empty placeholder showing who sent it and when stays in the conversation so the thread still reads in order. The stored copies of files that were attached to it are currently kept until your account is deleted.
  • Device sessions, with their IP address and device description, end when you sign out or revoke them, and otherwise expire seven days after they were last used.
  • One-time sign-in codes expire after ten minutes. Links in account emails expire after a short time or once used.
  • Files that fail the malware scan are never delivered and are moved to isolated storage instead.
  • Server logs are kept only as long as needed to diagnose problems.

When you delete your account

Your profile, uploaded files, sessions, two-factor settings, notifications and mutes are deleted, and you are removed from other people's block lists. In conversations that still have at least two members, the messages you sent are emptied the same way a deleted message is. A conversation left with fewer than two members — every one-to-one conversation, for example — is deleted entirely, including the other person's messages in it. Groups you created pass to another member.

We may keep information for longer where the law requires it, or where it is needed to deal with a legal claim or a serious violation of our Terms.

To exercise any of these, email from the address on your account. We will reply within one month, and we may need to confirm it is really you before acting. These requests are free.
